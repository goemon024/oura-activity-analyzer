Privacy Policy

Oura Activity Analyzer is currently a personal-use application.

The application accesses Oura data only after authorization by the Oura account holder.

Oura data is used for personal analysis of health and activity patterns.

Oura data is processed and stored locally on the user's computer and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Locally stored data can be deleted by the user at any time. Access to Oura data can also be revoked through the user's Oura account.
